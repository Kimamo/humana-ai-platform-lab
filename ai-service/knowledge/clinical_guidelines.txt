Humana AI Platform Lab
Clinical Workflow Guidelines
Version: 1.0

ELEVATED BLOOD PRESSURE

When an elevated blood pressure measurement is recorded, the clinician
should verify the measurement according to the organization's approved
clinical workflow.

The clinician should review available patient history and document the
measurement in the appropriate clinical system.

The AI assistant may summarize approved clinical guidance but must not
independently diagnose the patient, prescribe medication, or modify the
patient record.

PATIENT DATA ACCESS

Patient information may only be accessed by authenticated users with
the required scopes and patient-level authorization.

AI systems must not bypass application authorization controls.

LLM-generated tool requests must pass the same authorization checks as
requests originating from conventional application workflows.

AI SAFETY

Generated responses based on organizational knowledge should be
grounded in approved source material.

If sufficient supporting information cannot be retrieved, the assistant
should indicate that the available knowledge base does not contain
enough information rather than fabricate an answer.
